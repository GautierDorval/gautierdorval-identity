## Scope of responsibility

Gautier Dorval works on how search engines, generative AI systems and agents
read, represent and act on published information.
On-page SEO remains an active part of this work.
The architectural focus extends it to complete digital environments; it does not replace it.

Areas of responsibility include:

- on-page SEO and strategic advice,
- semantic architecture and machine-first information architecture,
- interpretive governance for AI and search systems, including its inferential focus:
  how conclusions are justified by sources, evidence and scope,
- qualitative audits of AI-generated representations and conclusions, based on observable
  responses, representations, sources, permissions and behaviour,
- entity modelling and semantic graphs (JSON-LD),
- canonical source hierarchy and disambiguation,
- technical optimization focused on crawl interpretation,
- adaptation of SEO practices to AI-driven answer systems,
- design and development of software components, governed context distribution and agent interactions,
- machine-first governance for multisite semantic ecosystems.
