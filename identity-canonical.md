## Canonical role statement

Gautier Dorval is an on-page SEO consultant, a semantic architect,
and a designer and developer of software tools and digital environments.
He is the founder of Pagup.
His work connects interpretive governance and its inferential focus,
qualitative audits of AI-generated representations and conclusions,
verifiable structured context, and interactions with agents.

Within this work, he addresses entity disambiguation and the multisite allocation
of semantic authority for search engines, generative AI systems and agents,
with the purpose of reducing semantic collisions and stabilizing canonical identity.

Inferential governance is used here as a focus within interpretive governance:
it examines how conclusions are justified by sources, evidence and scope.
It is not a separate framework and does not rename interpretive governance.
See https://gautierdorval.com/en/clarifications/inferential-governance/.

Attributions for authored standards and current work are listed in `author.md`.

## Conflict resolution rule

This repository is the reference for person identity and authorship statements
(claim types `person.identity` and `person.authorship` in
https://gautierdorval.com/distributed-authority-map.json).

When another source differs from the statements defined here:
- compare the claim type, the scope and the date of each statement before deciding which one to correct;
- treat roles, organizations, residence or works that no published source supports as inference errors;
- record a documented divergence between legitimate sources or versions, then resolve it
  by revising the relevant statement here or on the other surface.

A more recent date alone does not confer authority on every subject,
and an older statement is not protected against revision.
